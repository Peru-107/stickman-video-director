## Phase A — 导演预案

**English title:** *Stop Letting Overthinking Ruin Your Life*
**中文标题：**《别让“想太多”毁了你》

**核心观点：** 焦虑往往不是由现实造成，而是由尚未发生的失败想象造成；打破内耗最有效的方法，是立即完成一个微小行动。

**Hook：** 事情还没开始，大脑已经背上了一百种想象中的灾难。

**画面规格：** 16:9 横屏、黑色背景、白色火柴人。使用左—中—右横向调度、侧向跟拍、水平匹配剪辑和有意识的负空间。

**旁白：** 明亮、有力量的成年女性；自然美式英语；约 67 词；约 30 秒。

**强调色：**

- 高饱和焦虑紫：思想风暴和灾难想象
- 鲜明危险红：拒绝、牢笼和束缚
- 温暖行动金：道路、能量和成长

**BGM：** 0–10s 为 72 BPM 的低沉循环钢琴，加入时钟和心跳；约 0:15 开始蓄力，0:18 切换到 108 BPM 的鼓点、弦乐和明亮合成器。

**情绪曲线：** 疲惫恐惧 → 被困反转 → 行动释放。

## English VO transcript

Do you ever feel exhausted before you've even begun? Your mind pictures failure and rejection before you take a single step.

The more you chase certainty, the tighter fear wraps around your feet. But reality rarely hurts you most—your mind's imagined future does.

So interrupt the loop. Choose one tiny move and do it now. The moment you act, fear loses its power—and you take back control.

## Phase B — Omni Flash Production Package

### Global continuity block

- Three approximately ten-second clips; 16:9 horizontal; 720p target; 24 FPS; synchronized audio; approximately 30 seconds total.
- Pure black background with white stick figures and white line art.
- Circular head, no face, no hair, no clothing, stable proportions, uniform medium line weight.
- Saturated anxiety violet, vivid danger red, and warm action gold; use natural-language color names only.
- Bright, energetic adult female narrator with natural American English.
- Stage action across left, center, and right; use lateral tracking, horizontal match cuts, and deliberate negative space.
- Use rapid scene changes, kinetic motion-graphic transformations, and frequent visual events, while preserving an identical stick-figure design, constant line weight, and strict temporal consistency.
- Generate no visible writing. Keep every card, bubble, clock, meter, notification, and interface element icon-only; add optional phrases in post-production.

### Stitching guide

1. Clip 1 red prison bars extend down → Clip 2 transforms the same bars into vines.
2. Clip 2 gold beam spans the frame → Clip 3 opens with the same beam and A's hands still gripping the loosened vines.
3. Clip 3 holds the mountain, gold path, and clean overlay-safe negative space for approximately 12–18 frames; add the optional CTA in post-production.

Trim duplicate transition holds and use very short audio crossfades at each cut.

### Voice and music continuity

Independent text-only generations may vary slightly. Reuse the same voice or audio reference when available and repeat the identical narrator description in all prompts. For maximum consistency, keep the synchronized SFX from each clip but add one continuous external English voiceover and BGM track during final assembly.
